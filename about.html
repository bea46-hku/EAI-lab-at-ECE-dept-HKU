About

Our Mission

The Engineering AI (e-AI) Lab, developed by the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at the University of Hong Kong (HKU), is a cutting-edge research initiative.

We focus on advancing the frontiers of Artificial Intelligence, specifically engineered for hardware-software co-design, intelligent systems, and next-generation communication technologies.

Our goal is to foster innovation and nurture talent that will lead the AI revolution in engineering.

Through our research, we aim to bridge the gap between theoretical AI and practical engineering applications, creating solutions that impact industries and society.

Vision

Our Vision for the Future

The e-AI Lab envisions a future where artificial intelligence is seamlessly integrated into every aspect of engineering, creating intelligent systems that are efficient, sustainable, and accessible to all.

We aspire to be a global leader in AI engineering research, driving innovation that addresses the most pressing challenges in technology, society, and the environment.

  • Excellence in Research: Pushing the boundaries of AI through cutting-edge research and development.
  • Innovation & Impact: Creating real-world solutions that improve lives and industries.
  • Collaboration: Fostering partnerships with academia, industry, and government.
  • Talent Development: Nurturing the next generation of AI leaders and innovators.
  • Sustainability: Building AI systems that are environmentally responsible and energy-efficient.

By 2030, we aim to establish the e-AI Lab as a premier destination for AI engineering research in Asia, with a global network of collaborators and a track record of transformative innovations.

Policy

Policy & Governance Framework

The e-AI Lab is committed to conducting research and development activities that adhere to the highest ethical standards and regulatory requirements. Our policy framework ensures responsible AI development.

Research Ethics

  • Responsible AI: All research projects must consider ethical implications and societal impact.
  • Data Privacy: We adhere to strict data protection regulations and privacy-by-design principles.
  • Transparency: Our research methodologies and findings are published openly.
  • Inclusivity: We ensure our research benefits diverse communities and avoids bias.

Intellectual Property

  • Open Innovation: We support open-source initiatives where appropriate.
  • Patent Strategy: We protect innovations that have commercial potential.
  • Collaboration Agreements: Clear IP frameworks are established with all partners.

Industry Collaboration Policy

  • Transparent Partnerships: All collaborations are conducted with transparency and mutual benefit.
  • Knowledge Transfer: We facilitate the transfer of knowledge between academia and industry.
  • Conflict of Interest: Clear policies are in place to manage potential conflicts.

Environmental & Social Governance (ESG)

  • Sustainable Practices: We minimize environmental impact in our research activities.
  • Community Engagement: We actively engage with local communities and stakeholders.
  • Social Responsibility: We ensure our research contributes to social welfare and equity.

For detailed policy documents or inquiries, please contact us.
